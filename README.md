# Mikel McCormick
### Senior Software Engineer · Design Engineer · UX

I’m a software engineer with 18+ years of experience building web applications, shaping user experiences, and improving the systems behind them. My work spans frontend architecture, UX/UI design, design systems, and AI-powered products.

I’ve spent much of my career working between design and engineering. I enjoy taking an early product idea, working through the interaction and technical details, and turning it into software that is useful, maintainable, and good to use.

## What I Do

- **Frontend engineering:** Build production web applications, develop component architectures, and modernize legacy codebases.
- **UX and interaction design:** Turn product requirements into clear interfaces, thoughtful interactions, and practical user workflows.
- **Design systems:** Connect Figma design systems with component architecture to help teams build consistent experiences without slowing down product development.
- **AI-powered products:** Work on frontend architecture, prompt engineering, and LLM analysis, with a practical interest in where AI improves software development and where human judgment remains essential.
- **Technical collaboration:** Work closely with product managers, designers, and engineers to resolve ambiguous problems and move from concept to implementation.
